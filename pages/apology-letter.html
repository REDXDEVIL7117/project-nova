A Little Apology 💌

Something I probably should have said properly.

✦

Hey Janvi,

There is something I wanted to say properly instead of just leaving it somewhere between jokes, random messages, and all the chaos we've had.

Thank you for being there, for talking to me, for listening to me, and for becoming such an important part of my memories.

I also want to say I'm sorry.

I'm sorry for every time I teased you too much, said something that made you uncomfortable, or made you feel sad. Sometimes I probably went too far without realizing it, and I genuinely regret those moments.

You never deserved to feel bad because of something I said or did.

I also want you to know that having you in my life has meant a lot to me. Your presence gave me someone to talk to, laugh with, make random memories with, and just be myself around.

I don't think I always managed to explain how much I appreciated that.

So this is me finally saying it: thank you for being there.

And if I've ever made things difficult, uncomfortable, awkward, or sad for you, I'm genuinely sorry.

I hope when you look back at all these memories, you remember the good ones too. The stupid jokes, the random conversations, the chaos, and all the little moments that somehow became memories worth keeping.

Whatever happens as life keeps moving forward, I'm glad our paths crossed.

From,
Yash 💙

Some things are worth saying properly. ✦
